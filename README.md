## 🌟 About Karigar AI

Karigar AI is a voice-first, multilingual AI platform designed specifically for Indian artisans and traditional craft makers.

The platform helps artisans convert their spoken product stories into structured digital listings, create a verified Craft Digital Twin, generate marketplace-ready content, estimate fair pricing, and communicate with customers.

Karigar AI is designed to reduce the digital literacy barrier by allowing artisans to speak naturally in their preferred language.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many traditional artisans have valuable skills and unique handmade products, but face challenges such as:

- Limited digital literacy
- Difficulty creating online product listings
- Language barriers
- Difficulty marketing handmade products
- Lack of structured product information
- Difficulty calculating fair product prices
- Difficulty communicating with online customers
- Limited access to global marketplaces

Karigar AI addresses these challenges through a voice-first AI workflow.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Karigar AI provides an end-to-end digital assistant for artisans.

An artisan can:

1. 🎙️ Speak about their craft
2. 📸 Upload craft photographs
3. 🧠 Let AI understand the product
4. ✅ Verify important product attributes
5. 🧬 Create a Craft Digital Twin
6. ✍️ Generate marketplace listings
7. 💰 Estimate a fair price
8. 📣 Generate marketing content
9. 🌍 Translate and prepare multilingual content
10. 💬 Respond to customer questions using verified information

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🎙️ Voice-First Craft Input

Artisans can speak naturally about:

- Product details
- Materials
- Colors
- Crafting process
- Time required
- Product story

Karigar AI converts spoken information into structured product information.

---

### 🌍 Multilingual Support

Karigar AI supports a large collection of languages and dialects to make digital tools more accessible to artisans.

The interface includes language selection and multilingual content generation.

---

### 🧬 Craft Digital Twin

Each craft can have a structured Digital Twin containing verified information about the product.

It acts as a central source of product information for:

- Marketplace listings
- Translations
- Customer responses
- Pricing
- Marketing
- Product variants

---

### 🛡️ Claim Guard

Karigar AI helps protect listings from unsupported or unverified claims.

Only confirmed information is treated as trusted Digital Twin information.

This helps reduce misleading product descriptions.

---

### 📸 Product Photo Coach

Artisans can upload multiple craft photographs.

The system provides guidance for capturing useful product views such as:

- Front view
- Side view
- Texture
- Workshop
- Base mark

---

### ✨ AI Listing Studio

Generate and refine marketplace-ready content including:

- SEO title
- Product description
- Short summary
- Heritage story
- Feature bullets
- SEO tags

The listing can also be transformed using AI-assisted editing options.

---

### ✂️ AI Voice Editing

Artisans can refine their listing using simple commands and transformations such as:

- Make Shorter
- Expand Story
- Make Simpler
- Make Attractive
- Marketplace Ready

---

### ⚖️ Fair Price Assistant

Karigar AI provides an AI-assisted pricing workflow based on factors such as:

- Raw material cost
- Labor time
- Daily wage benchmark
- Overheads
- Craft complexity

The goal is to make artisan labor and material costs visible when preparing a price.

---

### 📣 AI Marketing Studio

Generate ready-to-use marketing content for:

- WhatsApp
- Instagram
- Online marketplaces
- Festive promotions

This helps artisans create promotional content without needing advanced digital marketing skills.

---

### 💬 Customer Reply Assistant

The Customer Reply Assistant generates responses using verified product information.

It is designed to avoid inventing unknown information such as unsupported delivery dates or certifications.

---

### 🏺 Craft Library

Artisans can maintain their craft information and product Digital Twins in a centralized library.

The platform also provides an option to export craft information as CSV.

---

### 🧠 Artisan Craft Memory

Karigar AI can store recurring craft preferences with explicit user confirmation, including:

- Primary craft cluster
- Frequently used materials
- Daily wage benchmark
- Artisan heritage information

This reduces the need to repeatedly enter the same information.
